# Creative Workshops in Menton and Nearby: Make Something to Take Home

There's something particularly satisfying about returning from a holiday with something you've made yourself.

Not another fridge magnet or a souvenir you could have bought anywhere, but a bowl shaped from clay, a bottle of perfume you designed or the knowledge of how to make fresh ravioli.

Menton and the surrounding French Riviera have several workshops where visitors can learn directly from local artisans, artists and food specialists.

Some take just an hour. Others offer a more substantial introduction to a traditional craft.

But not every workshop advertised online is a regularly available experience. Some are one-off events, private group activities or seasonal programmes with no future sessions announced.

For this guide, we've focused on established studios offering recurring classes, regularly bookable sessions or ongoing private lessons by appointment.

Here's what you can make, how to book and what you'll actually take home.

## 1. Kairòs Lab: make your own ceramic piece in Menton

Address: 6 Chemin du Rosaire, 06500 Menton

If you enjoy making things with your hands, Kairòs Lab is an excellent place to start.

Ceramic artist Martina Musetti runs a small studio where beginners can explore clay modelling, shaping and pottery techniques.

The atmosphere is more personal than a large classroom, with small-group and private formats.

### What can you make?

Depending on the workshop, you might shape a cup, bowl, small decorative object or another ceramic piece.

The studio also offers opportunities to try the pottery wheel during appropriate sessions.

### Regular workshops

Saturday group workshops: Two-hour themed sessions, normally once or twice per month, with up to six participants.

The currently listed group price is €55 per person. Materials and tools are included, but firing costs an additional €5 per piece.

Private individual lesson: Two hours, €150, including materials, equipment and firing.

Private couple's workshop: Two hours, €170 for two participants, including materials and firing.

Four-week course: Four lessons of two hours, priced at €200, with separate firing charges. This is particularly interesting for longer stays.

### Languages and age

Lessons can be delivered in French, Italian or English.

The studio also advertises children's workshops from age five. For standard group sessions, confirm whether the selected theme is appropriate for your child's age.

### What do you take home?

A ceramic item you've made yourself.

But not necessarily on the same day.

Clay needs drying and firing, and some pieces require glazing and a second firing. If you're visiting Menton for only a weekend, ask whether the studio can arrange later collection or shipping.

Don't assume shipping is included in the workshop price.

### How to book

Choose a published workshop date or contact the studio to arrange a private lesson.

[Official Kairòs Lab workshops](https://kairoslab.it/fr/pages/cour-de-ceramique)

Our recommendation: One of the best creative experiences in Menton, especially for couples or visitors staying at least a week.

## 2. Pasta Piemonte: learn to make fresh ravioli

Address: 34 Rue Partouneaux, 06500 Menton

Menton sits at the meeting point of French and Italian food traditions, making fresh pasta a particularly fitting skill to learn here.

Pasta Piemonte is known for its artisan pasta, including ravioli featuring Menton's famous lemons.

Its hands-on workshops introduce visitors to the techniques behind fresh pasta.

### What will you do?

You'll learn how to prepare dough, roll it, add fillings and shape ravioli by hand.

The experience concludes with a tasting, depending on the session format.

Workshops run in small groups of up to eight participants.

### Practical details

- Duration: Approximately 2 hours.

- Languages: French, English or Italian.

- Minimum age: 12.

- Booking: Choose an available session through the official booking page.

- Price: Confirm on the booking calendar; no universal workshop price is published.

- Availability: Scheduled sessions and private group arrangements.

**November notice:** The operator currently advertises its shop as closed from 2 to 16 November inclusive, reopening on 17 November. That notice does not independently confirm whether a private workshop can run during the closure: ask for explicit confirmation before planning a visit.

### What do you take home?

The clearest takeaway is a practical skill: making fresh pasta and filled ravioli.

A tasting is included, but the operator doesn't guarantee that every participant leaves with an additional parcel of fresh pasta.

If taking uncooked ravioli home is important, ask before booking.

[Book a Pasta Piemonte workshop](https://www.pastapiemonte.com/ateliers-pates-fraiches)

Our recommendation: Particularly appealing for food lovers who want an experience connected to Menton's local identity.

## 3. Painting and drawing with Saïd Berkane

Address: 13 Rue de la Conception, 06500 Menton

For something more personal, consider a lesson in an artist's studio in Menton's historic centre.

Saïd Berkane is a painter and decorative artist offering drawing and painting lessons for children and adults.

His work spans techniques including oil, watercolour, acrylic and pastel.

### What can you learn?

Lessons are personalised according to the student's level.

Depending on your interests and the format agreed with the artist, you may focus on drawing, colour, painting techniques or a particular subject.

This could be a rewarding choice if you're inspired by Menton's architecture, colours or Mediterranean scenery.

### Practical details

- Schedule: By appointment, Monday to Saturday.

- Season: Year-round.

- Language: French is the published language.

- Age: Children and adults; confirm the minimum age.

- Duration: Agree directly with the artist.

- Price: Confirm when booking.

- Materials: Ask what's provided and whether you need to bring anything.

### What do you take home?

Usually, a drawing or painting created during the lesson, although the medium matters.

An oil painting may need drying time before it can be transported. For a short holiday, watercolour or drawing may be more practical.

Confirm the planned medium and whether your work will be ready to carry away.

[Official tourist office listing](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/artiste-peintre-said-berkane-menton-fr-4674757/)

Our recommendation: Best for travellers looking for individual artistic attention rather than a fixed group activity.

## 4. Atelier Cuisine Niçoise: prepare regional recipes in Nice

Address: 2 Rue du Sénat, 06300 Nice

If you'd like to learn the food traditions of the French Riviera, Nice offers several established culinary schools.

Atelier Cuisine Niçoise is particularly interesting because it focuses on regional dishes rather than generic cooking demonstrations.

Situated near Cours Saleya, it has twelve equipped cooking stations.

### What will you make?

Classes cover traditional Niçoise recipes such as:

- Pissaladière

- Farcis niçois

- Gnocchi

- Capouns

- Sweet or savoury tourte de blettes

Recipes depend on the scheduled class and season.

The current classic course lasts about two and a half hours and starts at 9:30 a.m. You prepare two regional recipes and, depending on the format, eat them on site or take them away. Do not assume that an older summer-only pan bagnat programme is running on your travel dates.

### Practical details

- Language: French, with English explanations available from the chef.

- Age: Duo workshops accept children from 12 when accompanied by an adult. Private children's groups are accepted from 14.

- Duration: About 2.5 hours for the current classic course; other formats vary.

- Booking: Required.

- Price: The current classic-course listing gives €60 per person or €100 for a duo; confirm the selected date and format.

- What you take home: Your prepared food, unless you eat it during the session.

### How to book

Use the official booking link or call +33 4 89 06 48 01.

[Atelier Cuisine Niçoise information](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/sortir/activites-loisirs/atelier-cuisine-nicoise/)

Our recommendation: A strong option for visitors interested in local culinary heritage and traditional recipes.

## 5. Cuisine sur Cours: cooking for adults, children and families

Address: 75 Avenue Borriglione, 06100 Nice

Cuisine sur Cours offers an established programme of small-group cooking lessons throughout the year.

It's particularly useful for families because the school advertises separate formats for adults, teenagers, children and parent-child pairs.

### What can you make?

The programme changes according to seasonal ingredients.

Depending on the class, participants may prepare an individual dish, two dishes or a more complete menu.

There are introductory lessons, gourmet evenings and family-oriented sessions.

### Practical details

- Availability: Recurring classes throughout the year, normally Monday to Saturday.

- Language: English is listed among the available languages; confirm the selected session.

- Age: Some children's activities begin at age 3, with age restrictions depending on class.

- Duration: Varies.

- Price: Varies by format.

- Booking: Select a dated course from the school programme.

### What do you take home?

The recipes and techniques you've learned, together with any food the class format allows you to take away.

Some courses focus on preparing and eating a meal on site, so confirm whether takeaway portions are included.

[Official tourism listing](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/service-provider/cuisine-sur-cours-atelier-gastronomique/)

Our recommendation: A particularly useful choice for families and people interested in repeat cooking lessons during a longer stay.

## 6. Les Petits Farcis: English-friendly Riviera cooking

Address: 12 Rue Saint-Joseph, 06300 Nice

Les Petits Farcis is an established culinary school in Old Nice, known for its market-based cooking experiences and classes led by Rosa Jackson and her team.

Its programme includes French Riviera cooking, pastry and food-focused experiences.

### What can you learn?

Regular programme categories include market visits followed by cooking, pastry-making lessons featuring macarons and choux pastry, and other regional food experiences.

The school also runs recurring intensive programmes, including multi-day cooking courses.

For families, it advertises children's classes from age six, including a two-hour Wednesday format.

### Practical details

- Language: English or bilingual instruction.

- Age: Children's courses from age 6; other minimum ages depend on the activity.

- Duration: Two hours for the published children's class; adult classes vary.

- Booking: Required through the scheduled programme.

- Price: Depends on the course.

- Season: Year-round programmes, with class days varying.

### What do you take home?

Food you've made may be eaten during the class or packaged according to the session format.

More importantly, you leave with recipes and techniques you can recreate at home.

Confirm any physical takeaway with the organiser before booking.

[Les Petits Farcis tourist office listing](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/prestataire/atelier-de-cuisine-les-petits-farcis/)

Our recommendation: One of the most useful choices if English-language instruction is essential.

## 7. Fragonard: make your own fragrance in Èze or Grasse

Locations: Fragonard's perfume facilities in Èze-Village and Grasse

Perfume-making is one of the French Riviera's most distinctive hands-on activities.

Fragonard offers recurring fragrance-creation workshops at its facilities in Èze and Grasse.

For visitors based in Menton, Èze is geographically closer and can be combined with a visit to the medieval village.

### What will you do?

Depending on the workshop, you'll discover fragrance ingredients, learn how perfume is composed and create your own scented preparation.

Different programmes are available for different age groups and levels of interest.

### Practical details

- Language: French or English.

- Minimum age: 8 for mini workshops, 12 for the apprentice-perfumer programme.

- Duration: Depends on the chosen workshop.

- Price: Check the exact programme in the official booking calendar. Different workshop formats and language pages show different price bands; do not assume one universal price.

- Booking: Mandatory; choose the exact workshop, location, date and language.

- Season: Recurring sessions subject to the booking calendar.

### What do you take home?

Your own fragrance, in the bottle specified for the selected workshop.

Bottle sizes and compositions vary, so check the inclusions before payment.

### How to book

Use [Fragonard's official workshop calendar](https://usines-parfum.fragonard.com/en/workshop/). The [official FAQ](https://usines-parfum.fragonard.com/contact/) distinguishes the mini workshop from the cologne workshop. Check the named factory on your confirmation: a perfume-shop address is not necessarily your meeting point.

Our recommendation: An excellent shorter excursion for couples and families with children old enough to participate.

## 8. Galimard: create a personalised perfume in Grasse

Address: 5 Route de Pégomas, 06130 Grasse

Galimard's Studio des Fragrances provides a more extensive introduction to personal fragrance creation.

Participants work with a selection of 126 essences and receive guidance on building a balanced perfume.

### What will you do?

You'll learn about top, heart and base notes, then combine ingredients to develop a fragrance that reflects your preferences.

It's suitable for visitors who want more creative control than a very short introductory activity offers.

### Practical details

- Duration: Approximately 1 hour 45 minutes.

- Adult price: €65.

- Under-15 price: €55. The organiser currently states a minimum age of 8 and recommends an adult's presence for children.

- Languages: Select an available session language on the booking form; confirm your language before paying.

- Sessions: Usually 10 a.m., 2 p.m. and 4 p.m., subject to booking.

- Reservation: Required.

### What do you take home?

A personalised 100 ml perfume, a student-perfumer diploma and a recorded fragrance formula that can be used for future orders.

This is one of the clearest examples of a workshop where you take home an immediately usable item.

[Book a Galimard workshop](https://www.galimard.com/produit/atelier-de-creation-de-parfum-a-grasse-particuliers-2)

Our recommendation: Particularly appealing for perfume enthusiasts who want to spend more time creating a personal scent.

Molinard offers another established fragrance-creation experience in Grasse.

Its L'Atelier des Parfums gives participants access to 90 essences and professional guidance.

- Price: €84.

- Sessions: Generally 11 a.m., 2 p.m. and 4 p.m., subject to availability.

- Booking: Required.

- Season: Year-round, with specified holiday closures.

A 50 ml perfume, your exclusive formula and a diploma.

The formula allows you to reorder the fragrance later through Molinard.

[Official Molinard workshop listing](https://www.paysdegrassetourisme.fr/l-atelier-des-parfums-parfumerie-molinard-5724624/)

## A special consideration: when can you actually take your creation home?

This is more important than it might seem.

| Activity | Same-day takeaway? | Important consideration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pottery | Usually no | Drying, glazing and kiln firing take additional time |
| Drawing | Usually yes | Check materials and paper size |
| Watercolour | Often yes after drying | Protect the artwork during travel |
| Oil painting | Not always practical | Longer drying time |
| Cooking | Often eaten or taken away | Check storage and food safety |
| Fresh pasta | Depends on workshop | Uncooked pasta needs suitable storage |
| Perfume | Normally yes | Check bottle size and airline liquid restrictions |

For visitors flying home, airline cabin-baggage restrictions are especially relevant to 100 ml perfume bottles and any additional liquids.

And for pottery, ask about collection dates before paying. A ceramic piece that won't be fired until after your departure needs an agreed delivery solution.

## Which workshop is best for your trip?

### For couples

Consider a private pottery lesson at Kairòs Lab or a perfume-creation experience in Èze or Grasse.

Both involve making something personal rather than simply watching a demonstration.

### For families with young children

Kairòs Lab has children's pottery activities from age five, while Cuisine sur Cours offers some classes for younger children.

Fragonard's mini-perfume programmes accept children from age eight.

Always choose a specific age-appropriate session.

### For food lovers

Pasta Piemonte is particularly relevant to Menton's identity.

For broader regional cuisine, explore Atelier Cuisine Niçoise or Les Petits Farcis in Nice.

### For a rainy day

An indoor pottery session, cooking class or perfume workshop can be a good alternative to the beach.

A painting lesson is another option, particularly if you prefer a quieter activity.

### For a two- to four-week stay

Consider Kairòs Lab's four-week pottery course or a recurring series of cooking lessons.

Longer stays make it much easier to complete a multi-session programme and collect fired pottery before leaving.

## How far should you travel for a workshop?

Stay in Menton if you want pottery, drawing or pasta-making without organising a significant excursion.

Choose Nice for more culinary variety, especially classes focused on regional recipes.

Choose Èze for a perfume workshop combined with a visit to the medieval village.

Choose Grasse if fragrance creation is the main event and you'd like to explore the traditional perfume-making centre of the region.

Grasse is a more substantial excursion from Menton. Plan the return journey before reserving a late-afternoon session.

## Before booking: seven practical questions

Even established workshops can change their dates, instructors and booking conditions.

Before paying, check:

- Is this specific session available on your travel date?

- Will the class be taught in English or another language you understand?

- What is the minimum age for this exact format?

- Are materials and ingredients included in the price?

- What exactly will you take home?

- If your creation requires drying or firing, when can you collect it?

- What are the cancellation and rescheduling rules?

For private lessons, also confirm the minimum group size, total price and meeting address.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there pottery workshops in Menton?

Yes. Kairòs Lab offers recurring Saturday group workshops, private pottery lessons, children's activities and multi-week courses.

### Can you take a cooking class in Menton?

Yes. Pasta Piemonte offers hands-on fresh pasta and ravioli workshops. For a broader choice of Niçoise cooking classes, Nice has several established schools.

### Are creative workshops on the French Riviera available in English?

Several are. Kairòs Lab, Pasta Piemonte, Les Petits Farcis and the major perfume studios publish English-language options. Always confirm the language of your specific session.

### Can children participate?

Yes, but age restrictions vary. Kairòs Lab advertises children's ceramics from age five, while Fragonard has mini workshops from age eight. Some family cooking activities are available for younger children.

### Can you take your pottery home the same day?

Generally not if it requires kiln firing. Confirm the firing schedule, collection date and shipping possibilities before booking.

### Where can you make your own perfume near Menton?

Fragonard has workshops in Èze and Grasse. Galimard and Molinard also offer established fragrance-creation experiences in Grasse.

### Do you need to book workshops in advance?

Yes, for most of the experiences in this guide. Some operate only on scheduled dates, while others allow private bookings by arrangement.

### Are these workshops available throughout the year?

Most listed studios advertise ongoing programmes or regular bookable activities, but individual sessions may be seasonal or unavailable on particular dates. Check the official booking calendar rather than assuming daily availability.

## Final thoughts: a different kind of Riviera souvenir

The French Riviera is full of beautiful things to photograph.

But it can be even more memorable to create something yourself.

A hand-shaped ceramic cup, a sketch of Menton's old streets, the ability to prepare fresh ravioli or a perfume that reminds you of your holiday can become a more personal souvenir than anything purchased from a shop.

Start with the activity that genuinely interests you.

Then choose a workshop that fits your schedule, language and practical needs.

The best experience isn't necessarily the most elaborate one.

It's the one you'll still enjoy remembering long after you've returned home.
