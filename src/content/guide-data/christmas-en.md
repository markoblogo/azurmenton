Christmas on the French Riviera feels rather different from Christmas in northern Europe.

There may be palm trees beside the decorations, Mediterranean views behind the Christmas chalets and surprisingly mild afternoons for walking outdoors.

You can spend the morning in Menton, enjoy a festive market in Monaco or Nice later in the day and still return to your apartment for the evening.

But choosing where to go takes a little more planning than simply finding the nearest Christmas market.

Some events are suitable for an hour after dinner. Others deserve a whole afternoon. Some have ice skating and children's entertainment, while others are small village markets with a distinctly local atmosphere.

And not every event advertised for Christmas has confirmed its new-season dates.

This guide compares the most useful festive outings from Menton, with a clear distinction between confirmed 2026/27 programmes and events still awaiting an official announcement.

It also helps you plan the journey home, which is particularly important when travelling without a car.

## 3. Christmas in Menton: the easiest evening close to home

Main historic festival area: Mail du Bastion, Esplanade Francis-Palmero and Jardins Biovès.

If you're staying in Menton, a local Christmas evening has an obvious advantage: you can enjoy the atmosphere without worrying about trains, taxis or a long return journey.

The city's established Christmas programme typically includes festive chalets, decorations, food stalls and activities for children.

During the 2025/26 season, Menton offered a Christmas market, ice rink, fairground attractions, decorated gardens and a route of nativity scenes.

However, these are details of the previous edition.

### What's confirmed for 2026?

The City of Menton's general market information confirms a Christmas market in December.

As checked on 28 October 2026, the dedicated Christmas website still displays the programme running from 5 December 2025 to 4 January 2026. The municipality has also published a call proposing a village from 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027 on Esplanade Élisée Reclus near Borrigo, with proposed hours of 10:00–19:00. This preparatory notice is not a final visitor programme and does not confirm the locations or activities of the previous edition.

The 2026/27 opening and closing dates, skating programme and individual entertainment events have not yet been verified.

### How to plan it

If you're visiting during December, check the city's updated programme shortly before your trip.

If the market and waterfront attractions are operating, an easy plan is to have dinner first, then stroll towards the locations actually announced for this season. Mail du Bastion and Jardins Biovès are historical references, not a guarantee of this year's installations.

Allow an hour or two rather than trying to make it a major expedition.

### Best for

Couples who want a relaxed evening, families with small children and visitors who prefer to stay within walking distance of their accommodation.

## 4. Monaco Christmas Village: the strongest short evening trip

Location: Quai Albert Ier, Port Hercule, Monaco.

Confirmed dates: 4 December 2026 – 3 January 2027.

Monaco is particularly appealing for a festive evening from Menton.

It's close enough to reach without dedicating an entire day to travel, while Port Hercule provides a spectacular setting for lights, Christmas chalets and waterfront activities.

The official 2026 theme is La forêt enchantée, or The Enchanted Forest.

### What is confirmed?

Monaco's municipality has published the official 2026 Christmas Village operating regulations.

They confirm that the market will contain retail chalets, food stalls and amusement attractions.

The public opening schedule is:

| Day | Published hours |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday–Thursday | 11:00–22:30 |
| Friday–Saturday | 11:00–23:00 |
| 24 December | 11:00–19:30 |
| 31 December | 11:00–02:30 the following morning |
| 4 December opening day | From 16:00 |

Last entry is generally 30 minutes before closing.

The village closes for the season on 3 January 2027 at 22:00.

The full entertainment programme is still marked as forthcoming on the municipality's Christmas Village page.

### What about ice skating?

Monaco has separately confirmed that the Port Hercule ice rink will operate from 4 December 2026 to 7 March 2027.

Not every session is available for general public skating. Certain time slots are reserved for skating clubs and scheduled events.

The municipality also lists closures for ice shows on 18 and 19 December.

Check the official ice-rink timetable and price sheet before planning a skating session.

### Is Monaco suitable for children?

Yes, particularly for families looking for a festive market atmosphere and age-appropriate attractions.

However, amusement rides and skating activities may have separate age, height and supervision conditions.

Don't assume that every attraction is suitable for toddlers.

### How to get there without a car

Travel from Menton to Monaco-Monte-Carlo by regional train, then follow pedestrian routes towards Port Hercule.

Allow time for the walk between the station exit and the Christmas Village.

The return journey is the important part: check the train you intend to take before leaving Menton.

### Best plan

Choose Monaco for a two- to three-hour festive evening, especially if you want a Christmas atmosphere without travelling all the way to Nice.

## 5. Nice Christmas Village: the biggest festive outing

Location: Jardin Albert 1er and Place Masséna, Nice.

Confirmed dates: 27 November 2026 – 3 January 2027.

For a larger Christmas market, Nice is the strongest choice.

The tourist office confirms a festive village with more than sixty chalets offering food, gifts, crafts and seasonal specialities.

The surrounding city adds illuminated streets, shops, restaurants and additional Christmas programming.

### What can you expect?

The published programme includes seasonal food, regional products, Christmas gifts and artisan stalls.

Nice also has a Ferris wheel and other festive attractions in its wider Christmas programme.

The Christmas Village itself is free to enter.

Specific rides, purchases and some organised events may have their own charges.

### Hours and bookings

The official Nice tourist-office listing confirms that the Christmas Village opens daily from 27 November to 3 January.

However, the listing reviewed for this guide does not provide a full daily opening-and-closing timetable.

Check the city's Christmas programme again before planning an exact evening arrival.

The City of Nice also publishes separate dated performances and activities for children, including some with age-specific admission.

### Best for families

Nice is particularly attractive for families with older children who enjoy browsing markets, trying festive food and combining several activities in one outing.

For younger children, it may be more comfortable to arrive during the afternoon rather than travel home late.

### How to get there

Take the regional train from Menton towards Nice.

From Nice-Ville station, continue on foot or by local tram towards Place Masséna and Jardin Albert 1er.

Allow time for the local connection, particularly with children.

### Best plan

Choose Nice for half a day plus an early evening.

You can explore the old town in the afternoon, enjoy an early dinner and visit the Christmas Village after dark.

Official information:[ ](https://www.nice.fr/noel-a-nice/)[Nice Christmas Programme](https://www.nice.fr/noel-a-nice/)

## 6. Carnolès: Saint-Nicolas festivities for children

Location: Carnolès Market area, Roquebrune-Cap-Martin.

Confirmed date: Sunday, 6 December 2026.

Published hours: 09:00–18:00.

Admission: Free.

For families with younger children, Carnolès is one of the most practical alternatives to a large Christmas market.

The published programme includes traditional games, entertainment, performances and seasonal activities.

### Children's highlights

The official listing describes wooden games, a small fairground, musical entertainment and a Saint-Nicolas-themed programme.

Selected scheduled activities include:

- 10:00: Children's storytelling and performance.

- 11:30: Arrival of Father Christmas figures on Solex mopeds.

- 14:00: Character parade.

- 15:00: Children's performance.

- 15:45: Saint-Nicolas and gift distribution.

- 16:30: Traditional music and dancing.

The event is outdoors and subject to suitable weather.

### Why choose Carnolès?

Unlike a late-evening Christmas market, this is an activity-rich daytime outing.

It's a good choice for younger children who may be tired by the evening.

The location is also close to a railway station, making it attractive for visitors without a car.

### Best plan

Arrive late morning, enjoy the programme, have lunch or a snack, then return to Menton before dinner.

Official event:[ ](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/les-villages-saint-nicolas-roquebrune-cap-martin-fr-6862934/)[Les Villages Saint-Nicolas](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/les-villages-saint-nicolas-roquebrune-cap-martin-fr-6862934/)

## 7. Sospel: a traditional Christmas market in the mountains

Location: Place Garibaldi, Sospel.

Confirmed date: Sunday, 6 December 2026.

Published hours: 10:00–16:00.

Admission: Free.

If your ideal Christmas outing involves a historic village rather than a major seaside market, consider Sospel.

The atmosphere is more traditional, with local surroundings and a mountain-town setting.

### What is confirmed?

The tourist office has published the market for 6 December 2026, with a rain contingency date of 12 December.

It's described as a family-friendly Christmas market near the village's riverside centre.

### Is it an evening trip?

Not really.

The published closing time is 16:00, so this works better as a late-morning and afternoon visit.

### Getting there

Check current Zest bus and regional transport options for the date.

Although Sospel is not geographically far from Menton, public transport frequency can be limited on Sundays.

Don't assume that an afternoon return connection will be convenient.

### Best plan

Visit the market, explore a little of historic Sospel and return during daylight.

Official event:[ ](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/marche-de-noel-sospel-fr-3389197/)[Sospel Christmas Market](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/marche-de-noel-sospel-fr-3389197/)

## 8. Castellar: a small Christmas celebration above Menton

Location: Place Georges Clemenceau, Castellar.

Confirmed date: Sunday, 13 December 2026.

Published hours: 10:00–18:00.

Admission: Free.

Castellar offers a more intimate alternative to Monaco and Nice.

The official programme includes artisan stalls, local products, Father Christmas, children's activities and festive entertainment.

The village's stone streets and mountain setting add character to the outing.

### Why choose it?

Castellar is especially appealing if you enjoy local traditions, small markets and a slower pace.

For children, the event listing includes seasonal activities and a bouncy structure.

### Walking considerations

Castellar is a historic hilltop village.

Even where the event area itself is listed as wheelchair-accessible, surrounding streets can involve slopes, steps and uneven paving.

Visitors with pushchairs or limited mobility should check the exact approach.

### Getting there

Zest line 6 is the main bus route to investigate from Menton.

But Sunday service needs particular attention.

If you're travelling without a car, confirm both outward and return journeys before committing.

### Best plan

Arrive late morning, enjoy the festivities and lunch, then return to Menton in the afternoon.

Official event:[ ](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/journee-de-noel-a-castellar-castellar-fr-6868786/)[Christmas Day in Castellar](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/offres/journee-de-noel-a-castellar-castellar-fr-6868786/)

## 9. What about Christmas in Italy?

Ventimiglia, Bordighera and Sanremo can offer attractive Christmas atmospheres, but Italian events follow their own municipal calendars.

As of 27 October 2026, we could not establish a sufficiently complete and reliable programme of Christmas markets, hours and individual entertainment dates for all three destinations.

That means they should be treated differently from Nice and Monaco.

### When does an Italian trip make sense?

If you already want to spend a day in Ventimiglia or Sanremo, festive decorations can be an enjoyable addition.

But don't travel specifically for a Christmas market based solely on last year's dates or a generic travel-directory listing.

Check the relevant municipality's current calendar and confirm the location and opening time of the event you intend to visit.

### Travel considerations

Ventimiglia is reached by coastal train from Menton.

For Sanremo, you may need a connecting Italian regional service.

Tickets, operators and return times require separate verification.

If the Italian Christmas programme remains unconfirmed, a daytime visit for food and sightseeing is a more dependable plan than a late-night festive outing.

## 10. Three Christmas outings after an early dinner

A festive evening doesn't need to finish at midnight.

These scenarios are particularly useful for couples and families based in Menton.

### Evening A: Stay in Menton

Best for: Toddlers, tired travellers and short outings.

Have dinner at the apartment or a confirmed open restaurant around 17:30–18:30.

If the 2026 market is operating, enjoy the decorations and stalls in the officially announced locations; Mail du Bastion is a reference from earlier editions.

Walk towards Jardins Biovès if its festive installations are accessible.

Return to your apartment without using public transport.

Why it works: Very flexible, with an easy exit whenever children become tired.

Verification required: Menton's actual 2026/27 programme.

### Evening B: Christmas at Port Hercule, Monaco

Best for: Couples and families with school-age children.

Have an early dinner in Menton or Monaco.

Travel to Port Hercule for the Christmas Village.

Spend one or two hours browsing chalets, enjoying the lights and visiting suitable attractions.

Return on a preselected train.

Why it works: Monaco provides a substantial festive atmosphere without needing a full-day excursion.

Verification required: Train home and any paid activity you want to use.

### Evening C: Christmas lights in Nice

Best for: Adults and older children.

Travel to Nice in the afternoon.

Have an early dinner near the centre.

Explore the Christmas Village around Jardin Albert 1er and Place Masséna.

Return to Menton on a confirmed evening service.

Why it works: The largest choice of food, market stalls and city-centre atmosphere.

Verification required: Market closing time and actual return connection.

## 12. Getting home: the most important part of an evening trip

The coastal train makes Nice and Monaco attractive from Menton.

But it shouldn't be treated as a guaranteed late-night service.

### Engineering works through 11 December 2026

SNCF has published works affecting the Nice–Monaco–Menton–Ventimiglia railway.

During the current works period, rail traffic is interrupted from approximately 22:00 to 06:00 on Sunday-to-Thursday nights.

Replacement road services may operate, but they do not necessarily follow the same route or depart from the ordinary station platform.

The works are currently scheduled through 11 December 2026.

This creates a particularly important constraint for the first part of the Christmas season.

### What should you do?

Check the outward journey and return separately.

Use the actual date, not a generic weekday timetable.

If a replacement coach is offered, confirm the boarding location, destination and whether your ticket is valid.

Allow time to walk from the Christmas venue to the station or coach stop.

For families, consider returning earlier rather than relying on the final available service.

### What about after 11 December?

Don't assume that the entire holiday railway timetable is free from disruption simply because a published works period has ended.

Check SNCF's live journey planner and any subsequent notices.

### What about the mountain villages?

For Sospel and Castellar, the problem is often service frequency rather than night works.

Set your return departure before leaving Menton.

If no convenient connection exists, choose an event closer to the coast or arrange a suitable vehicle in advance.

## 14. Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve

The three dates require different planning.

### Christmas Eve: 24 December

Monaco's Christmas Village has a confirmed early closing time of 19:30.

Other markets, restaurants and shops may also close earlier than usual.

Arrange a restaurant reservation well in advance, or purchase ingredients for a meal at your apartment.

### Christmas Day: 25 December

Don't assume that every Christmas market or attraction follows ordinary hours.

Even if an outdoor site remains accessible, individual stalls and activities may be closed.

Public transport may use holiday schedules.

For a dependable day, enjoy a coastal walk and organise a confirmed meal rather than building the entire itinerary around shops.

### New Year's Eve: 31 December

Monaco's Christmas Village regulations permit opening until 02:30 the following morning.

However, this does not confirm every concert, DJ programme or fireworks display.

It also doesn't guarantee public transport home at that hour.

If you want to stay for midnight festivities, investigate a prebooked transfer or suitable accommodation near the event.

Don't plan the return journey on assumptions.

## 16. Five checks before leaving for a festive evening

- Is the event confirmed for the current season?

- Is it open at the time you intend to arrive?

- Are the particular rides, performances or skating sessions available?

- Does the activity suit the children's ages?

- How will you get back to Menton, including after any transport disruption?

If you can't answer the fifth question, choose an earlier return or a different destination.

A Christmas outing should end with pleasant memories, not an unexpected night stranded at a railway station.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Christmas market is best near Menton?

Nice offers the largest confirmed market, while Monaco is particularly convenient for a shorter evening. Menton's own event is the easiest local option once its current programme is confirmed.

### Are Christmas 2026 dates confirmed for Menton?

The municipal call proposes a village from 4 December to 3 January at Élisée Reclus, but this is not a final visitor programme. The dedicated website still displays the 2025/26 edition during our check. Exact activities and the final programme require confirmation.

### When is Monaco's Christmas Village open in 2026?

From 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. The municipality has published the market's operating schedule and the Enchanted Forest theme.

### Is Nice's Christmas Village free?

Yes, entry to the market is free. Individual attractions, rides, food and purchases can cost extra.

### What is the best Christmas activity for young children?

The confirmed Saint-Nicolas celebrations in Carnolès on 6 December offer daytime games and performances. A short local outing in Menton can also be more manageable for toddlers.

### Can you visit Nice's Christmas market without a car?

Yes, using regional trains and local transport, but confirm the return journey, particularly during scheduled night engineering works.

### Are there Christmas markets in hilltop villages?

Yes. Sospel and Castellar have confirmed events on 6 and 13 December 2026 respectively. Public transport may be limited, especially on Sundays.

### Can you visit a Christmas market on 25 December?

It depends on the destination and individual operating arrangements. Don't assume that ordinary daily hours apply to every stall or attraction on Christmas Day.

### Can you return from Monaco after midnight on New Year's Eve?

Don't assume that a normal regional train will be available. Check actual holiday transport and arrange a suitable alternative if necessary.

## Final thoughts: choose the Christmas outing that fits your evening

The French Riviera offers several very different ways to enjoy Christmas.

You can keep things simple with a festive walk in Menton.

You can head to Monaco for an illuminated harbour and Christmas chalets.

You can spend half a day exploring Nice's larger market and city-centre decorations.

Or you can choose a smaller village celebration where local traditions are the main attraction.

The most enjoyable option isn't necessarily the biggest one.

It's the one that suits your available time, your travelling companions and your journey home.

Choose the experience first, confirm the programme, then organise the return.

That leaves you free to enjoy the festive atmosphere without turning a pleasant evening into a logistical challenge.
