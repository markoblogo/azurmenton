# Winter guide source review: 27 October 2026

Claim-level review only; resort openings, snow, road access and every museum
are not independently verified. Check operators close to the visit date.

- [Mountain escapes](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/mountains-snow-skiing-near-menton):
  [Zestbus network maps](https://www.zestbus.fr/se-deplacer/telecharger-les-plans/)
  list line 15 Menton-Castillon-Sospel and line 25 Menton-Ventimiglia-Breil-Tende.
  Its network map identifies lines 19 and 23 as summer services. Check the
  [current timetable](https://www.zestbus.fr/telecharger-les-horaires/) and exact
  return date rather than assuming all trips run daily or serve every stop.
  [TER works](https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/se-deplacer/info-trafic/travaux/nice-monaco-vintimille-nice-breil-tende)
  and the [national transport data feed](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/resources/83196)
  report the Nice-Breil-Tende and coastal interruptions on 10 November and until
  around 17:30 on 11 November 2026, with limited replacement options. Specific
  tickets, connections, and replacement capacity were not tested.
- [Museums](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/museums-in-menton-nice-monaco):
  [Menton city](https://www.menton.fr/musee-de-prehistoire-regionale.html)
  lists Tuesday and several holiday closures for the Regional Prehistory Museum.
  [Marc Chagall Museum](https://musees-nationaux-alpesmaritimes.fr/chagall/en/practical-information)
  lists winter hours for 1 November 2026 to 30 April 2027, a midday closure,
  Tuesday and holiday closures, and early closing on 24 and 31 December. Some
  galleries can close; check directly. Other museum hours and any exceptional
  closures were not reviewed in this pass.

No new places or illustrations were introduced. Public links give travellers
the latest source rather than guaranteeing future seasonal operations.
