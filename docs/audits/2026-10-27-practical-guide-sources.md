# Practical guide source review: 27 October 2026

This is a bounded, claim-level review, not a verification of every venue, price,
opening time or location in these guides.

- [Electric car charging](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/electric-car-charging-menton):
  [Menton's charging information](https://www.menton.fr/Bornes-de-recharge-vehicules-electriques-menton.html)
  and [city parking page](https://www.menton.fr/-stationnement-menton-.html) list
  municipal charging locations and EVzen. The parking page mixes old free-charging
  language with later paid rates; neither should be treated as a current tariff.
  Connector availability and charging speeds were not verified live. Corrected
  the apartment parking claim in all locales using the owner's clarification:
  Panoramic Sea View Studio has no private parking.
- [Petrol stations](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/petrol-stations-menton):
  [Intermarche Hyper](https://www.intermarche.com/magasins/01296/menton-06500/infos-pratiques)
  and [Intermarche Super](https://www.intermarche.com/magasins/01298/menton-06500/infos-pratiques)
  list stations at 45 Avenue Saint-Roman and 91 Route Val des Castagnins. Both
  pages list 24-hour fuel service; recheck before travel rather than guaranteeing
  uninterrupted service. BP's linked station page could not be fetched in this
  review; this is not evidence it has closed. Corrected the same apartment
  parking claim in all locales.
- [Laundry](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/laundry-laundromats-menton):
  [Intermarche Hyper](https://www.intermarche.com/magasins/01296/menton-06500/infos-pratiques)
  lists a self-service laundry at 45 Avenue Saint-Roman. The [tourist office](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/menton-et-le-littoral/les-ports/)
  lists laundry among Port de Garavan services, but does not confirm its operator,
  hours or public access. Added these references and retained checks for current
  access, machines and payment. Other laundry businesses and estimated prices
  were not independently verified in this pass.

No new locations were created. These checks do not establish that every claim in
any of the three guides is current; no guide-wide verification status was changed.
