# Luggage storage and airport taxi review: 27 October 2026

This is a claim-level review, not a verification of all operators, dates or prices.

- [Luggage storage guide](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/luggage-storage-in-menton-monaco-and-nice):
  [Radical Storage near Menton station](https://radicalstorage.com/luggage-storage/menton/menton-train-station/luggage-storage-menton-train-station)
  requires online booking and shows date-dependent closures, not a fixed daily
  schedule. Its booking page mentions a protection charge; final prices and the
  address should be confirmed in the booking flow. The [Plage du Marche tourism
  listing](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/useful-information/all-practical-services/luggage-storage-plage-du-marche-menton-en-5870334/)
  dates from 2025 and its hours differ from the [operator's listing](https://radicalstorage.com/fr/consigne-a-bagages/menton/gare-de-menton);
  do not promise either timetable. The operator lists the [Intermarche locker](https://radicalstorage.com/luggage-storage/menton/menton-train-station/luggage-storage-intermarche-hyper-menton)
  as 24-hour, small/medium luggage only, with booking required; physical access
  outside the shopping centre's hours was not independently tested.
- [Nice-Ville SNCF](https://www.garesetconnexions.sncf/en/stations-services/nice/services-shops/consignes-bag-mobile)
  lists Bagmobile in the east hall and publishes hours and prices; a separate
  [SNCF FAQ](https://www.garesetconnexions.sncf/fr/faq/services/quel-est-prix-du-service-consignes-bagages-combien-coute-consigne-gare-000001069)
  quotes a different pricing scheme, so guests should check with the service.
  The [Nice tourist office](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/service/bagmobile-consigne-a-bagages/)
  lists the Rue Baralis lockers and their 2026 hours. [Nice Airport](https://www.nice.aeroport.fr/en/services/pratical-services/luggage-storage)
  specifies EUR 19 for *less than* 24 hours, not up to and including 24 hours.
  The rest of the guide's facilities and all live bookings remain unverified;
  its guide-wide status is therefore `needs_verification`.
- [Airport transfer guide](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/how-to-get-to-menton-from-nice-airport):
  [Nice Airport](https://www.nice.aeroport.fr/en/directions/taxis) places official
  taxi ranks at A3 for both terminals. Its published fixed-fare table does not
  include Menton; this does not establish the actual fare or that a private
  operator cannot provide a fixed quote. Removed the unsupported price range and
  added the [prefecture's taxi regulations](https://www.alpes-maritimes.gouv.fr/Demarches/Taxis-VTC-2-et-3-roues/Taxis2).
  No individual private-transfer provider or 2027 fare was verified.

No new locations or illustrations were introduced.
