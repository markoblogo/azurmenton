# Pharmacy guide source notes: 27 October 2026

- [Nice Airport](https://www.nice.aeroport.fr/en/services/shops/pharmacy) lists
  a public pharmacy before security at Terminal 2, level 2, near D2. Hours are
  published but are not 24-hour; verify before travel.
- [French business register](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/825245640)
  and the [regional authorisation](https://www.prefectures-regions.gouv.fr/irecontenu/telechargement/83690/539803/file/Recueil-r93-2021-093_24%20juin%202021.pdf)
  indicate Pharmacie Riviera at 7-7 bis Avenue de Sospel. An older tourist
  listing gives 17 Avenue de Sospel; that [old establishment closed](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/etablissement/82524564000016).
  Current opening hours and entrance coordinates were not verified.
- [French business register](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/925248205)
  lists Pharmacie Garavan at 15 Porte de France. Current hours and coordinates
  require a separate check. Existing central cards are reused; not all listed
  pharmacies were independently reviewed in this pass.
- [Assurance Maladie](https://pharmacie-de-garde.ameli.fr/) offers an on-duty
  pharmacy search, but a live result for Menton was not verified because the
  service requires JavaScript. A [Menton pharmacy](https://pharmaciementon.wixsite.com/pharmacie)
  also points to 3237; pricing and night access depend on the service and case.
  Emergency guidance directs serious cases to 15 or 112, not to a pharmacy.

The three new locations are explicitly excluded from the map until coordinates
are checked. The guide cover and three place images are awaiting owner-supplied
illustrations. This is not a full clinical, opening-hours or provider audit.
