# Car-free transport source review: 27 October 2026

This is a bounded review of routes, fares and published stops; it is not a live
booking, an accessibility assessment, or a guarantee of a particular service.

- [Airport access](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/how-to-get-to-menton-from-nice-airport):
  [Line 80's operator](https://www.niceairportxpress.com/en/) publishes direct
  airport-Monaco-Menton journeys, but its timetable also shows departures that do
  not continue to Menton. Menton-origin services in the displayed timetable
  arrive at Terminal 2. The published single ticket was EUR 21.40 and the driver
  purchase surcharge EUR 0.40 per person when checked; group and eligible-card
  fares have separate conditions. Ticket price and availability may change.
  [Nice Airport](https://www.nice.aeroport.fr/en/directions/public-transport)
  lists the airport stops; the operator's current timetable remains the source
  for the direction and service chosen.
- [Local transport](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/public-transport-in-menton):
  [Zestbus](https://www.zestbus.fr/les-nouveautes-du-reseau-zestbus-au-06-juillet-2026)
  lists a EUR 2 one-hour ticket and EUR 1 central shuttle from 6 July 2026.
  [ZOU](https://zou.maregionsud.fr/fiches-horaires-des-bus-zou/alpes-maritimes)
  lists 600 and 601 Nice-Monaco-Menton timetables. This does not confirm every
  stop, frequency, or fare for those regional lines.
- [Menton without a car](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/menton-without-a-car):
  [SNCF Connect](https://www.sncf-connect.com/train/horaires/menton/vintimille)
  showed direct Menton Garavan-Ventimiglia trains on the checked date; station,
  route and times differ by service. [TER](https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/se-deplacer/info-trafic/travaux/travaux-entre-nice-vintimille)
  lists overnight Nice-Ventimiglia works through 11 December 2026 and replacement
  options with eligibility conditions. Refer travellers to live train and works
  information rather than promising times, free travel or a particular connection.

No new locations or illustrations were introduced. Other transport claims and
future fares remain subject to individual verification.
