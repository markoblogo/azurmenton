## Source Check — 2 November 2026

- [Pass prices](https://www.frenchrivierapass.com/en/your-pass/french-riviera-pass-all-inclusive/): €30 / €45 / €65. [FAQ](https://www.frenchrivierapass.com/en/faq/): consecutive validity, single visits, no ordinary public transport, standard pass price regardless of age.
- [Kérylos](https://www.villakerylos.fr/billetterie/) and [Ephrussi](https://www.villa-ephrussi.com/en/prepare-your-visit/rates): €15 / €18; joint ticket €24, valid eight days. Ephrussi states on-site purchase. Do not inherit obsolete pandemic notices from the English rates page.
- [Matisse](https://www.musee-matisse-nice.org/en/practical-information/): €10; municipal four-day pass €15, child/reduced admission conditions. Exact visit dates and closures remain the visitor's responsibility.
- [SNCF Nice](https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/trajet-menton-nice) and [Monaco](https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/trajet-menton-monaco): standard one-way €7.70 / €4.10. Return figures are arithmetic, not separate return-ticket offers or promises of a specific departure.
- Material correction: the [pass homepage](https://www.frenchrivierapass.com/) advertises a €58 Galimard value, but its [activity catalogue](https://www.frenchrivierapass.com/en/your-pass/french-riviera-pass-all-inclusive/savings-calculator/) names Pause Cologne. [Galimard](https://www.galimard.com/visite-guidee-eze) and [its linked booking](https://www.galimard.live/produit/atelier-pause-cologne-a-eze-village-particuliers) price that short experience at €18. Use €18, not €58. Workshop-only pass costs €12 more; the three-day set totals €52 separately versus €65 with pass (€13 more), not €92 and €27 saved.
- Age/reservation conflict: Galimard says age five and recommends reservation, minimum two creations online; the [tourism listing](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/culture/galimard/) says age six and walk-in. Keep this uncertainty explicit and ask visitors to confirm applicable pass-holder conditions; no organiser messages sent.
