# Guide scenario improvements: 27 October 2026

- [Italian Riviera day trip](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/italian-riviera-day-trip-from-menton):
  replaced oversimplified station counts and journey durations with checking the
  actual departure station, connection, return and required documents. [SNCF](https://www.sncf-connect.com/train/horaires/menton/vintimille)
  lists direct services on the checked date, but not every journey or station is
  identical. [Trenitalia](https://www.trenitalia.com/) and [TER works](https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/se-deplacer/info-trafic/travaux)
  are linked for date-specific onward travel. No connection or fare is guaranteed.
- [Family guide](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/menton-with-kids-family-guide):
  added a local rainy-day option and an optional Monaco museum day. [Menton city](https://www.menton.fr/musee-de-prehistoire-regionale.html)
  gives museum hours and closures; [Monaco Oceanographic Museum](https://musee.oceano.org/en/practical-info/)
  suggests approximately two hours and lists its closures. Check programmes,
  accessibility and opening times for the chosen date.
- [Luggage storage](https://azurmenton.com/en/guide/luggage-storage-in-menton-monaco-and-nice):
  added a city-by-city decision summary and booking checklist. Provider hours,
  prices and availability remain subject to the [previous source review](2026-10-27-luggage-taxi-sources.md)
  and the current provider booking flow. Host storage is not guaranteed.

No new locations or images were added. These are targeted improvements, not a
complete verification of the three guides.
