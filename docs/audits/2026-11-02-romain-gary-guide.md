## Editorial Decisions

- Remove SEO/service preamble, trailing Russian editorial recommendations and
  the literal `<escape>` artifact. Combine short source paragraphs for reading;
  preserve the book discussion, two walks, travel example, adaptations and FAQ.
- Distinguish autobiographical fiction, documented addresses and filming places.
  Remove the precise arrival month and plaque unveiling date from the owner draft
  rather than imply they were independently confirmed in this pass.
- The original document says researched 1 November. Links inspected 2 November:
  [university walking bibliography](https://rim.univ-cotedazur.fr/items/show/2921),
  [university Mermonts record](https://rim.univ-cotedazur.fr/items/show/2923),
  [publisher's English editions](https://www.ndbooks.com/book/promise-at-dawn/),
  [school contact address](https://www.lycee-massena.fr/nous-contacter/),
  [municipal literary context](https://www.nice.fr/ressources/magazine-special-nice-se-met-a-louvrage/),
  [Film France adaptation record](https://www.filmfrance.net/en/once-upon-a-time/promise-at-dawn/).
  University items 2919 and 2924 could not be retrieved through the research tool;
  retained as further-reading references, not represented as newly verified.
- Mermonts and the school are exterior-only stops. Negresco is an operating hotel,
  not an unrestricted literary attraction. Rue Shakespeare's precise apartment
  entrance and Scarouget interior access remain unverified. Walking allowances
  are editorial estimates, not measured routes or accessibility guarantees.
- Reuse Cours Saleya and Roquebrune village. Add six honest missing-image cards;
  no substitute images. Three eligible fixed sites have explicit coordinate
  exclusions; streets/neighbourhoods remain outside the current point taxonomy.
- Link the literary guides, market guide and car-free/day-trip materials in both
  directions. The existing Nice day-trip section gains the promenade card and
  a short Gary option. No unrelated event or venue links for volume.
