# Ski season source review: 27 October 2026

- [Auron](https://hiver.auron.com/) announces ski-area opening on 5 December 2026.
- [Isola 2000](https://isola2000.com/en/isola2000-in-wintertime/) announces resort
  and ski-area reopening on 5 December 2026. Another page still contains an
  undated 6 December opening; check the live resort announcement before travel.
- [La Colmiane's official sales page](https://colmiane.axess.shop/) forecasts
  weekends from 5 December and daily operation from 19 December to 21 March
  2027. The [tourist-office listing](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/station/domaine-skiable-de-la-colmiane/)
  gives different dates, so the operator's plan remains provisional.
- [Valberg](https://www.valberg.com/les-activites/sports-dhiver/ski-alpin-snowboard/domaine/),
  Turini Camp d'Argent and Limone Piemonte: no independently verified official
  2026-2027 ski-area opening date found in this pass. A ski school's or shop's
  opening date does not prove ski-area opening.
- [Nice tourism office line 91](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/service/bus-ligne-91-nice-auron/)
  lists regular Nice Grand Arenas-Auron service in 2026. A separate 2026-2027
  dedicated ski-bus timetable, winter fares and connections from Menton were
  not verified. Older snow-bus pages must not be treated as this year's schedule.

All opening plans depend on conditions and can change; lift, trail, snow and
road status must be checked close to departure. No new places were created.
