## Discovery and cross-check sources

- [Menton Riviera & Merveilles agenda](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.fr/sorganiser/agenda/tout-lagenda/): regional tourism office; includes Menton, Roquebrune, Beausoleil and inland towns.
- [City of Menton agenda](https://www.menton.fr/%2B-une-agenda-%2B.html): municipal local events.
- [Nice Events](https://niceevents.nicecotedazur.org/): city cultural listings, including smaller events.
- [Monaco city events](https://mairie.mc/evenements): check eligibility restrictions for resident- or school-only events.
- [Riviera dei Fiori](https://rivieradeifiori.travel/en/events/): regional discovery; corroborate individual occurrences with the venue or organiser.

## Editorial process

1. Review a rolling 7-14 day window, prioritising Menton and accessible nearby towns; record source URL and date checked.
2. Confirm the specific occurrence, date, local time, venue, access, age restrictions, booking and price where known. Unknown stays unknown; an old or generic weekly schedule is not a confirmed event.
3. Compare title, date, venue and organiser with published events and current candidates. Link existing place and relevant guides when editorially appropriate; do not create duplicate event or venue cards.
4. Identify schedule changes and cancellations before publishing; exclude past events from current listings. Check late return transport where relevant without promising a service.
5. Record findings for human editorial review. No automatic publication, outreach, newsletter subscription or copying of third-party images. Ask the owner for illustrations when new cards need them.

This registry proposes a review cadence only; it does not configure a scheduled job. Respect source terms and access limitations.
